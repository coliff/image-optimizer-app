Privacy policy

Image Optimizer for Windows

Image Optimizer doesn't collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or ads, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

The images you add are optimized on your own computer. While it works, the app copies each image to a private temporary folder on your computer and deletes the copy when it's done. Your settings are saved on your computer, in the app's own data folder.

When you install or update it, the Microsoft Store handles the download under Microsoft's privacy statement. Opening the GitHub link in Settings opens your web browser, where GitHub's privacy statement applies.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
